How to read this. Metric is the only mandatory choice. Every other axis appears only if the selected route physically has it — an axis flagged in orange does not exist on that branch and is skipped, never rendered as an empty dropdown.
Readiness. UI DEMO READY means a forecast source contract is resolved and the series can be displayed. ANALYTICS READY additionally requires a resolved actuals source, which does not hold everywhere: 5,438 leaves have observed actuals, 449 are NO_DATA and 496 remain unresolved.
What is real and what is not. Taxonomy, entity lists, entity counts, route contracts and source columns are real, from the E1–E11 evidence. The plotted series and the per-entity observation counts are simulated and will be replaced by E11_leaf_data_profile.csv.
E11 counts 6,383 logical leaves because Region identity there also carries Environment; per-route entity counts are distinct Region / Forest / Forest × SKU values.
